DevNetwork API + Cloud + AI Hackathon 2026 — submission gallery

These are the projects the event labelled Winner, beside projects listed on the same page without
that label. They are kept verbatim because they do a job no summary can: they are the calibration
anchors the ranking compares candidate ideas against, and paraphrasing them would replace the
evidence with an opinion about the evidence.

The unlabelled projects are the gallery's own neighbours on page one, not a fair sample of
everything that did not win. Two of them sit squarely on the winning theme with clean lines, which
is the most useful thing here: it shows that theme and phrasing alone did not decide the outcome.

`ideate gallery` writes more of these. The more events represented, the less any single event's
sponsors and tracks skew what the comparison treats as a winner.

WINNERS
- AegisFlow: Everyone is selling you an autonomous procurement agent. AegisFlow makes the opposite bet: the AI does the four hours of investigation, then stops. A human keeps the pen.
- compare.ai: Live AI analysis for every product, telling you what to KEEP and what to SKIP.
- VOWS&VIBE: Vows & Vibe is a collaborative bridal styling app with AI try-on, personal color guidance, shared feedback, and group previews so every look works beautifully together
- Chancery: Power of attorney for AI agents. A human signs what the agent may commit to; every irreversible act is checked against that signed document and refused, out loud, with the clause it broke.

- Signet: Invoice fraud works because paper cannot prove who sent it. Signet has the sender sign the payment fields and publish the key on their own domain, so anyone can check it with dig and openssl.
- DealForge: DealForge turns messy commercial handoffs into trusted, signed agreements by separating AI interpretation from commercial authority, human approval, and Doctavian execution.
- LaunchKit: Your domain already replaces Bitly, Linktree & Google Workspace ($56/mo). LaunchKit makes it easy: AI naming, branded links, email, DNS, security & brand protection — no agency needed.
- DomainTwin AI: Detect DNS drift, explain it with AI, restore trusted state through Name.com, and prove recovery with deterministic verification.

- PantryProof: PantryProof uses Nutrient and SerpApi to turn fragmented recall evidence into cited, human-reviewed actions and a replayable closure packet for every partner pantry.
- AccessForm: Call. Talk. Your form is filled.
- Unmet: Reviews in, ranked gaps out. Built on nine SerpApi engines: what app users are begging for, what's breaking for the businesses on your street, and whether Google's AI answer cites you.
- DepositCheck: Before you wire a rental deposit, drop in the listing's photo. Google Lens through SerpApi finds everywhere that exact image already lives, and names the other address it really belongs to.

- Nerve: The nervous system for your device fleet — AI-native IoT telemetry monitoring that turns a firehose of signals into a short list of incidents worth your attention.
- Time-Out: Surgeons pause before every incision. Med spas don't. Time-Out checks who is injecting, what they're using, and whether the patient understood, and refuses when the evidence isn't there.

LISTED WITHOUT A WINNER LABEL
- Dream It: Dream-It is an Agentic AI ecosystem that autonomously optimizes your productivity, health, and wealth. It features an Autopilot Taskmaster, a clinical Speech module, and a proactive Finance manager!
- Claim Ready: Claim Ready is a supplier-recovery evidence firewall that proves every euro before a claim reaches the supplier or finance.
- Sensentia: Xano-powered AI platform that turns human signals and context into real-time insights and adaptive actions through secure cloud APIs.
- Wearwheather: WearWeather is a dressing rehearsal for the day you actually have.
- fellowagent: Autonomous CLI in Go: uses AI to process PDFs, send Foxit eSign envelopes from local files, and watch/download signed docs automatically.

- Pulse: Pulse turns live web search, events, local listings, and search momentum into trustworthy nightlife recommendations for where to go right now.
- Mandate Inbox: French companies must use e-invoices now, but leftover PDF bills still arrive. Mandate Inbox reads those PDFs, stops the ones with wrong totals, and tells you what to do with foreign suppliers.
- Countersign: The agent proposes, the human countersigns, and both are on the record.
- WishLens: WishLens uses Perfect Corp to turn live skin analysis and controlled visual targets into a shopper-chosen preference that dynamically reshapes the product shelf.

- DealProof: AI can draft the deal. DealProof proves it
